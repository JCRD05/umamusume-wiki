Current events!
“Trainer Aptitude Test” is here!

The Aptitude Test is a new event in Umamusume Pretty Derby that has you completing a set of tasks in order to get points and earn a high score. Learn more about the Trainer Aptitude Test and what rewards to prioritize.

Event rewards

  • Gold Crystal Shards 10,000 points
  • Rainbow Crystal Shard 5,000 points
  • 450 Carats 4,500 points
  • Trainee Scout Tickets x3 4,500 points
  • Support Card Scout Tickets x3 4,500 points

The point reward system

Your goal in the Trainer Aptitude Test is to get as many points as possible. This means you'll have to meet the stat requirements and win several races to get a better chance at getting an A. Focus on meeting these goals and don't worry if the end result isn't optimal. You can see how it works through the following sheet:

Event bonuses

Top 30 Umamusume Can Be Borrowed!

The top 30 Umamusume who got the highest scores during the event will show up in the borrow list and can be borrowed an unlimited number of times! You can use them to your advantage so your Umamusume can gain an upperhand with better sparks and skills.
